# Illinois Digital Asset Tax Repeal Campaign

**Goal:** Repeal the Digital Asset Tax Act (Public Act 104-0468) via HB 5798 before it takes effect January 1, 2027.

**Window:** Fall veto session, est. November 12-21, 2026.

---

## The Problem

Illinois SB 3019, signed by Governor Pritzker on June 16, 2026, imposes a **0.2% privilege tax on the total value of every digital asset transaction** — not profits, the entire amount. No other asset class is taxed this way. No other state does this. The projected revenue ($60M) is 0.11% of the state budget. The cost: driving an entire industry out of Illinois.

## The Solution

**HB 5798** (Rep. John M. Cabello, R-68) — a clean, two-sentence repeal:

> *"The Digital Asset Tax Act is repealed. Effective immediately."*

---

## Three-Prong Strategy

### 1. Lobby
Direct engagement with legislators, especially the 20 members of the House Revenue & Finance Committee where HB 5798 will be heard. We need 11 votes in committee (7 Republicans are likely yes; we need 4 of 13 Democrats). Focus on Chairman Curtis Tarver II, who controls the hearing schedule.

### 2. Grassroots
Constituent pressure during the July-October recess. Town hall attendance, phone calls, letters, and testimony from real people in key districts. The message: this tax hurts your constituents and drives economic activity out of your district.

### 3. Federal/Legal
Constitutional arguments (Commerce Clause, Internet Tax Freedom Act, Equal Protection) and federal-level pressure (CFTC Chair Selig's "sin tax" characterization). If repeal fails, these arguments become the basis for a legal challenge.
