# Campaign Graphics to Create

## Shareable Infographics

### 1. "Death by a Thousand Cuts" — The Bank Account Analogy
- Side-by-side: "Your Bank" vs "Illinois Crypto Tax"
- Bank: $0 fee to move money between accounts → Crypto: 0.2% of TOTAL VALUE every move
- Show a $10,000 portfolio making 10 transfers: $200 gone
- Show DeFi with 50 daily contract interactions: devastating

### 2. "Not Your Profits — Your Principal"
- Visual showing: Capital Gains Tax (tax on profit) vs DATA (tax on every transaction's total value)
- Show: sell stock at a loss → no tax. Move crypto at a loss → STILL taxed 0.2%

### 3. "What $60M Costs Illinois"
- The tax raises ~$60M. Illinois budget is $56B. That's 0.1%.
- What it costs: OnionDAO (400 people, month of economic activity), crypto jobs, tech companies relocating
- Visual scale comparison

### 4. "$60M vs What OnionDAO Brings"
- Direct spending: venue, restaurants, catering, entertainment, accommodation
- 400 attendees × [average spend] = [X]
- Plus: free education, community building, talent pipeline

### 5. "Who Supports Repeal?"
- Logos/names: CFTC Chair Selig, Coinbase CEO, Michael Saylor, CCI, Illinois Blockchain Association
- Quote cards from each

## Tools You Can Use

- **Canva** — free, drag-and-drop, has templates for all social sizes
- **Figma** — more control, free tier works
- **CapCut** — free video editing for the animated counter
- **Unsplash/Pexels** — stock photos for backgrounds (but real OnionDAO photos > stock)

## Brand Elements

- Colors: match the dashboard (#0F172A dark, #FFD700 gold, #EF4444 red, #22C55E green)
- Fonts: system sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro, or similar clean modern)

- Campaign name: "Repeal DATA" or "Save OnionDAO"
