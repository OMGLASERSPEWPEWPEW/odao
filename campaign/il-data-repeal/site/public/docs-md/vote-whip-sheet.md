# Vote Whip Sheet — HB 5798 (Repeal Digital Asset Tax Act)

## Overview

**Bill:** HB 5798 — Repeals the Digital Asset Tax Act (35 ILCS 195/Act rep.)
**Sponsor:** Rep. John M. Cabello (R-68)
**Committee:** House Revenue & Finance Committee
**Committee size:** 20 members (13 Democrats, 7 Republicans)
**Needed for committee passage:** Simple majority (11 votes)
**Full House passage:** Simple majority (60 of 118 votes)

**Strategy:** All 7 Republicans are likely yes votes. We need **at least 4 Democrats** on the committee to advance. On the full House floor, the original SB 3019 passed 73-41 — meaning at least 32 reps voted against it or abstained. We start with those.
