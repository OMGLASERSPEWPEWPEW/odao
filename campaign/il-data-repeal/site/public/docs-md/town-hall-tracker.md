# Town Hall Tracker — DATA Repeal Campaign

## How to Find Town Halls

Town halls and constituent meetings happen during the legislative recess (roughly July through October, and between session weeks). Here's how to find them:

### Online Resources
1. **ILGA Member Pages:** Visit [ilga.gov](https://www.ilga.gov/) and search for your legislator. Their profile page often lists upcoming events.
2. **Legislator websites:** Many reps maintain personal websites with event calendars separate from ILGA.
3. **Social media accounts to follow:**
   - `@ILSenDems` — Illinois Senate Democrats
   - `@ILHouseGOP` — Illinois House Republicans
   - `@ILHouseDems` — Illinois House Democrats
   - `@ILSenateGOP` — Illinois Senate Republicans
   - Individual legislator Twitter/X, Facebook, and Instagram accounts
4. **Local community calendars:** Check your village/city website, library event boards, and community center listings.
5. **Patch.com / local news sites:** Often list town hall events for local areas.

### Direct Contact
1. **Call district offices.** The single most reliable method. Ask: "When is the Representative's next town hall or constituent meeting?" If none scheduled, ask: "How can I request a meeting?"
2. **Sign up for legislator newsletters.** Most have an email list. Sign up on their website or call the office and ask to be added.
3. **Contact local Democratic/Republican party offices.** They often know about informal constituent events.

### Tips for Tracking
- Start checking **weekly** beginning in July
- Some legislators announce town halls only 1-2 weeks in advance
- "Coffee with your Rep" and "Listening Sessions" count — any public constituent meeting is an opportunity
- If no town halls are scheduled, **request a private meeting** through the district office. Groups of 3-5 constituents can often get 15-30 minutes.

## What to Do at a Town Hall

### Before
- [ ] Review the legislator's voting record on SB 3019 (did they vote yes or no?)
- [ ] Prepare your 2-minute testimony (see `testimony-template.md`)
- [ ] Bring copies of the one-pager to hand out
- [ ] Coordinate with others attending — don't all ask the same question
- [ ] Arrive 15-30 minutes early to sign up to speak

### During
- [ ] Ask a clear, direct question: "Will you support HB 5798 to repeal the Digital Asset Tax?"
- [ ] If they waffle, politely press: "Can I follow up with your office for a definitive position?"
- [ ] Take notes on their response (exact words matter for the whip sheet)
- [ ] Be respectful regardless of response — other constituents are watching
- [ ] If possible, record (check if recording is allowed; most public town halls are recordable)

### After
- [ ] Update this tracker immediately
- [ ] Update the `vote-whip-sheet.md` with any new position information
- [ ] Send a follow-up email/letter thanking the legislator for their time
- [ ] Post a recap on social media (see `social-media-kit.md` for templates)
- [ ] Report back to the campaign coordination team
