### Post-Veto Session — Contingency Planning

| Scenario | Action Required |
|----------|----------------|
| **HB 5798 passes committee and floor** | Celebrate. Monitor Senate. Push for Governor's signature. |
| **HB 5798 passes committee, fails floor** | Analyze floor vote. Identify persuadable "No" votes. Prepare for lame-duck session (December). |
| **HB 5798 never gets a committee hearing** | Escalate pressure on Chairman Tarver. Engage Senate allies to push companion bill. Consider legal challenge. |
| **Lame duck session (December 2026)** | Last chance before Jan 1. New legislature sworn in January — bill would need to be re-filed. Extremely tight window. |
| **January 1, 2027 — Tax takes effect** | Shift to legal challenge strategy. File Commerce Clause / ITFA lawsuit. Continue building coalition for repeal in 105th General Assembly. |
