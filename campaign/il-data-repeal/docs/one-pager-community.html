What is the Onion DAO?

This past June, we hosted a 30-day, pop-up technology village in downtown Chicago. We brought together 400 builders, developers, and curious locals for a month of free, hands-on technology education. It served as a vital "third space" designed to build workforce skills and keep tech talent in Illinois.

  • The Space: A shared downtown floor operating as a 24/7 coworking and socializing space with free high-speed internet.
  • The Curriculum: Week-long, intensive workshops covering artificial intelligence, software development, 3D printing, and hardware soldering.
  • The Community: Fully catered lunches to ensure no attendee went hungry, paired with evening networking events — from riverfront concerts to community yacht outings — designed to build lasting professional bonds.

The Impact of Free, All-Ages Tech Education

At the end of the month, we held an "open gifting" ceremony where attendees nominated peers who impacted them. The stories from our attendees prove why these spaces are a necessity for Illinois:

"I met a kid named Christian on the first day who told me he felt like he 'didn't learn anymore' in traditional environments. On the last day, he stood up and gifted one of our AI workshop teachers, David Steinberg, just to thank him for letting him pick his brain for a month. That is what happens when you give people a free space to build." — Deric Ortiz, Onion DAO Attendee & Workshop Lead
