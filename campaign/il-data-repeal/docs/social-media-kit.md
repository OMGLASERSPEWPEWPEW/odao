## Engagement Guidelines

- **Reply to every comment** with substance. Don't argue, inform.
- **Quote-tweet allies** to amplify reach.
- **Tag legislators** when sharing town hall reports (but don't spam).
- **Don't be partisan.** This passed with bipartisan votes and the repeal needs bipartisan support. Frame as policy, not politics.
- **Share personal stories** — these get more engagement than policy arguments.
- **Use the thread format** for complex arguments on Twitter/X.
