# OnionDAO Economic Impact on Chicago

## Purpose

This document quantifies the economic value that OnionDAO and the broader crypto community bring to Chicago. Use these numbers in testimony, op-eds, and meetings with legislators to demonstrate what Illinois stands to lose under the Digital Asset Tax.

---

## Event Overview

OnionDAO is a month-long hacker residency and community gathering in Chicago that brings **400+ attendees** from across the country and internationally to live, work, eat, and build in the city.

**Duration:** ~30 days
**Location:** Chicago, IL
**Attendees:** 400+ (developers, artists, founders, community members)
**Recurrence:** Annual
