# OnionDAO Economic Impact on Chicago

## Purpose

This document quantifies the economic value that OnionDAO and the broader crypto community bring to Chicago. Use these numbers in testimony, op-eds, and meetings with legislators to demonstrate what Illinois stands to lose under the Digital Asset Tax.

---

## Event Overview

OnionDAO is a month-long hacker residency and community gathering in Chicago that brings **400+ attendees** from across the country and internationally to live, work, eat, and build in the city.

**Duration:** ~30 days
**Location:** Chicago, IL
**Attendees:** 400+ (developers, artists, founders, community members)
**Recurrence:** Annual

**Context that makes this devastating:**
- Chicago just raised hotel taxes to **19%** — the highest in the country — specifically to fund Choose Chicago's $40M/year convention attraction program
- Choose Chicago offers bid fees, incentives, and room night rebates to attract events. OnionDAO came *for free* and brought 400 people
- The city's tourism economy generates ~$20B in annual economic impact
- Las Vegas ($457M), Orlando ($116M), and LA ($62M) spend heavily to attract these events. Chicago is now competing against them while the state taxes away the events they're trying to attract

**The punchline:** OnionDAO's conventional tax contribution to Chicago almost certainly exceeds what the Digital Asset Tax would collect from community DAOs. Illinois is killing the golden goose.

### Competitive Comparison

Events that cities fight to attract (and don't tax the underlying industry):
| Event | Attendees | Duration | City Support |
|-------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| SXSW (Austin) | 400,000+ | 10 days | City provides infrastructure, police, road closures. No tech industry tax. |
| Consensus (Miami) | 20,000+ | 5 days | Miami actively courts with crypto-friendly policy |
| Solana Breakpoint | 3,000+ | 3 days | Cities compete to host — Lisbon, Amsterdam, Abu Dhabi, Singapore, London |
| OnionDAO (Chicago) | 400+ | **30 days** | State is taxing the industry that funds it |

OnionDAO is unique: a **month-long** event with sustained daily spending, not a 3-day conference. The per-attendee economic impact over 30 days likely exceeds any comparable tech event per capita.
