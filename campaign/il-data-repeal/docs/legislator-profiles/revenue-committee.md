## Research Checklist

For each legislator, complete the following:

- [ ] **SB 3019 roll call vote** — Pull from ILGA (ilga.gov → legislation → SB 3019 → Roll Call)
- [ ] **District office contact info** — Pull from ILGA member profiles
- [ ] **Stand With Crypto grade** — Check standwithcrypto.org/politicians
- [ ] **Social media accounts** — Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram
- [ ] **Campaign finance records** — Check Illinois Sunshine (illinoissunshine.org) for tech/finance sector donors
- [ ] **Previous tech/crypto votes** — Search ILGA for digital asset, blockchain, or fintech-related legislation
- [ ] **Competitive district status** — Check 2024 election margins; competitive-district members are more responsive
- [ ] **Known crypto constituents** — Identify OnionDAO attendees, meetup organizers, or crypto businesses in each district
- [ ] **Term status** — Verify whether each member is up for election Nov 2026 or retiring
