- **Historical salons:** Originated 16th c. Italy, flourished in France 17th-18th c.
- **Model:** Informal gatherings hosted by salonnières, reciprocal conversation among equals (not lectures)

### Csikszentmihalyi's Systems Model of Creativity
- The guy who coined **"flow"** — also wrote *Creativity* (1996)
- Three interlocking components:
  - **Domain** — symbolic rules, knowledge, procedures (the "what")
  - **Field** — gatekeepers who decide what's valid (the "who decides")
  - **Individual** — the creator who absorbs domain knowledge and produces novelty
- Creativity = individual learns from domain → makes something new → field accepts/rejects it → accepted work changes the domain
- **Relevance to Ferris:** The club IS the field — a curated group of AI builders who validate each other's work. The AI assistant learns the domain. The members are the individuals. The salon model collapses all three into one room.
- **Slide (IMG_4076):** "What were the patterns of success?" — arch/dome visual showing individuals rising through the Field (gatekeepers) into the Domain (mode of symbols). "Mihaly's team of PhDs studied 100+ people who changed their Domain."

### The Dome Metaphor — Brunelleschi & Florence Cathedral
- **Santa Maria del Fiore** — cathedral sat with a 150-ft hole in its roof for ~100 years (~1296–1420)
- Nobody could figure out how to span 144 ft without scaffolding — not enough timber in Tuscany, no cranes capable
- **Brunelleschi** solved it (1420–1436): self-supporting herringbone brick pattern, double-shell construction
- Still the **largest masonry dome ever built** — 4M+ bricks, 25,000 tons
- **The metaphor:** The domain existed (cathedral architecture). The field said it was impossible. One individual cracked it.
